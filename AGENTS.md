# Instructions for agents

This repository takes an NXP FRDM-IMX8MPLUS to a running Debian system with Debian's own
kernel. Read `README.md` first: it says what this is and has the installation steps.

## Installing it for someone

Stop and ask before each of these, naming exactly what you are about to do:

- **Writing the SD card.** `dd` to the wrong device destroys a disk. Show the person the
  output of `lsblk` and have them confirm the device name. Never guess it.
- **Shutting down or restarting the board.**
- Plugging cables, swapping the card and power cycling are theirs to do; say plainly what
  you need and wait.

Nothing here changes the eMMC: removing the card boots NXP's image again.

Say what you did, what you checked and how, and what you could not check.

## Changing the repository

- No names of private machines, addresses, accounts or passwords, in files or in commit
  messages. This repository is public.
- Commit messages: a short subject and, for anything of substance, a body saying what
  changed and why. No attribution or "generated by" trailers (`.claude/settings.json` turns
  them off for Claude Code).
- `imx8mp-frdm-eqos-ptp.dtso` is the overlay as submitted upstream; do not change it here.
- After changing a `.dtso` or the `Makefile`, rebuild the `.dtb` and `.dtbo` files with
  `make` and commit them with the change; after changing `boot.cmd`, rebuild
  `boot.scr` with the `mkimage` command in it.
- Pushing, creating repositories, and anything else that others can see: only when the
  person has said yes to that action.
